HOW IT WORKS

Three moves. That's the whole tutorial.

STEP 01
PICK YOUR AVATAR

No sign-up, no password. Choose a pixel walker and you’re on the roster.

STEP 02
START A WALK

Grab a free treadmill and hit start. The timer runs while you stroll.

STEP 03
EARN & ADVANCE

Streaks and achievements stack up while the whole team advances along a shared route.

POWER-UPS

Everything a walking team needs. Nothing it doesn't.

LIVE WALK TIMER

A running clock for every active walk, visible to the whole team.

DAILY STREAKS

Walk every workday to keep the flame alive. Miss a day, start over.

ACHIEVEMENTS & RECORDS

Longest walk, most walks in a week, personal bests — all tracked.

TEAM ROUTE MAP

Every minute walked moves the team along a shared virtual route.

TELEGRAM BOT

Link the team bot once — walk reminders and team updates land right in Telegram.

AI MOTIVATION HINTS

Playful AI-generated nudges to get you walking — with a graceful fallback when offline.
